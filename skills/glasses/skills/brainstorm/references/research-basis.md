# Research basis and limits

Isaksen and Gaulin (2005), [“A Reexamination of Brainstorming Research:
Implications for Research and Practice”](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249826503_A_Reexamination_of_Brainstorming_Research_Implications_for_Research_and_Practice),
reviews barriers to brainstorming and reports an exploratory human-group
study concerning facilitation and idea generation. The paper discusses
deferring judgment and reports greater idea fluency in facilitated conditions.

This provides a rationale for separating idea exploration from a later
evaluation phase. It does not establish that an AI facilitator improves an
individual's reasoning or decisions. Applying findings from human groups to
human-agent interaction is a design hypothesis, not a validated outcome. Do
not describe Brainstorm as a scientifically validated decision method.
